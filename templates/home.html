How It Works in 3 Quick Steps

Fast turnaround because fresh cooked food has only hours before it spoils

1
Donor Posts Extra Food

Hotels, caterers, or households enter food type, portions, pickup address, and a safe expiry window (under 12 hrs) in less than 60 seconds.

2
Receiver Claims in 1-Click

NGOs and verified volunteers view available listings with real-time countdowns and claim instantly. Double-claim protection prevents wasted trips.

3
Pickup & Deliver

The receiver navigates to the unlocked pickup address, collects the fresh food, delivers it to shelter homes or people in need, and marks it delivered.
